# Tidewell launch brief

## Product

Tidewell is an iPhone app that shows tide times, swell and wind for the stretch of coast you fish from, on one screen.

## Facts you may use

- Covers 1,284 tide stations in the US and Canada. Say "1,284" or "more than 1,200". Never round up.
- Tide predictions run 7 days ahead. Swell and wind forecasts run 72 hours ahead.
- Accuracy: predicted high tide is within 6 minutes of the observed time at 90% of stations (checked against NOAA and CHS records, Sept 2026). This is a claim about 90% of stations, not "90% accurate".
- Save up to 10 spots. Saved spots work offline, including the 7 day tide table.
- Free for 14 days, then $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year. No ads.
- Launches 18 November 2026 on the App Store, iPhone only. Nothing else is announced; do not mention other platforms.
- Download link: tidewell.app/get

## Not true, do not say

- Tidewell does not use AI or machine learning.
- There is no free forever tier.
- No ratings, reviews or user counts exist yet.

## Audience

Shore anglers and kayak anglers on the US and Canadian coasts who plan trips around the tide. Name them.

## Voice

- Talk to the reader as "you".
- Plain and calm, like a friend who fishes. Sentences under 20 words.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hashtags.
- End the launch post with one clear call to action that uses the download link.

## Banned words

game-changer, revolutionary, ultimate, unlock, seamless, effortless, best-in-class, cutting-edge, AI, smart

## App Store subtitle

At most 30 characters including spaces. It must not repeat the app name "Tidewell" (the store shows it already) and follows the banned words list.
